# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

First public release.

- Focus sessions per course and objective, with apps and sites closed, shielded, warned about or judged against your goal
- A local judge (Ollama) that asks "is this work?" when what is on screen drifts from what you said you are doing
- Day score (plan · calm · kept · closed), the end-of-session tally, course XP and levels, streaks and freezes
- Week calendar with an optional ICS feed, a daily shutdown that runs past midnight (the study day ends at 4 am)
- Hard mode: a session that relaunches the app if it is quit
- Pinned apps (Discord by default) are never blocked, only budgeted, and can be unpinned
